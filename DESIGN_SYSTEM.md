# VerseBill design system

Paste this into a new chat to reload taste.

VerseBill UI is a premium payment/invoicing product first and a Web3 app second. Soft lavender background `#FAF9FF`, white rounded cards, purple `#6D35F2` primary actions, Inter, official logo at `public/image.png`. Visual reference: `public/image copy.png` — soft mobile payment screens, not neon crypto. Success is green `#16A866`. Signature element: Payment verified on-chain. Do not flood the UI with gradients, glass, or DeFi charts.

## Laws

- Cards 20–24px radius, buttons/inputs 12–16px (use 16–22px), pills 999px
- Soft shadow + thin lavender border + white surface
- Buttons shrink on hover (`scale-95`), never grow
- Logo: official mark + VerseBill word in the navbar
- Customer pay: invoice → confirm → verified
- Never mark paid without backend verification

## Reject

Black crypto dashboards, neon, giant gradient cards, glassmorphism, trading charts, TVL/APY, fake success copy, “All Set. Well Done!”
